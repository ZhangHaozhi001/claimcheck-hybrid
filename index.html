    How it works

    1. On-device screen (Gemini Nano). The claim is screened locally in Chrome — no network, works offline, nothing leaves the device.
    2. Hybrid routing. Long documents, low screen confidence, red flags or an unsettled screen escalate automatically.
    3. Cloud deep check (Firebase AI Logic). Gemini in the cloud applies the ClaimCheck methodology: primary sources first, verbatim quotes, negative space reported, graded verdict.
    4. Archive. Every check is saved to Firestore (local storage in demo mode) so verdicts stay auditable.
